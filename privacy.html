Privacy policy

Last updated 23 October 2026

The short version: we don't keep your drafts. To rewrite a post we send it to an AI provider and pass the result straight back to you. To enforce the free daily limit we keep a random visitor ID, your IP address and a count of your rewrites for up to 48 hours. Accounts are optional; if you sign in, we also keep your email address. We don't sell data, show ads, or ask for your LinkedIn login.

Who runs this site

Simple LinkedIn Rewriter is an independent project and is not affiliated with LinkedIn. For any privacy question or request, email xiarui091@gmail.com.

What we process and why

The drafts you rewrite

When you press Rewrite, your draft and the tone you picked are sent to our server and on to an AI provider, which writes the new version. We don't save drafts or rewrites in a database or in our logs.

We use two providers:

  • Groq handles rewrites first. It processes your text under the Groq privacy policy.
  • Google Gemini API is used only if Groq is unavailable. We use Gemini's free tier, and under Google's terms content sent to free-tier services may be used to improve Google's products and may be read by human reviewers.

Because of this, don't paste confidential information, or personal details about other people that they haven't agreed to share.

Counting free rewrites

Free visitors get 5 rewrites a day. To count them we use:

  • a cookie called slr_vid holding a random ID, kept for up to a year so we can recognise your browser;
  • your IP address, so clearing cookies doesn't reset the limit for everyone on a network.

Each day's counts are stored in our database (Upstash Redis) against that ID and IP address, and are deleted automatically after 48 hours. If the database is unavailable we count in a signed cookie called slr_use instead, which expires after 2 days.

When someone enters an invite code, we also count attempts per IP address for one hour to stop people guessing codes.

Invited access

If you enter a valid invite code without signing in, your browser tab keeps a signed pass in session storage. It's deleted when you close the tab, and expires after 12 hours either way. If you're signed in, invited access is saved on your account instead and lasts until you sign out. Either way you can end it anytime with "Switch to free plan".

Accounts (optional)

You can use the site without an account. If you choose to sign in:

  • Supabase runs sign-in and stores your account: your email address, when you signed up and last signed in, and technical sign-in records such as IP address for security. See the Supabase privacy policy.
  • You sign in with a link that Supabase emails to the address you enter. There are no passwords.
  • Your daily rewrite count and any invited access are linked to your account ID in our database, so they follow you across devices. Daily counts are deleted after 48 hours.
  • Your browser keeps your sign-in session in local storage until you sign out.

To delete your account, email us from the address you signed in with and we'll remove it.

Hosting, fonts and analytics

  • Vercel hosts the site. Like any web host it processes technical request data, such as your IP address and browser type, to deliver pages. See the Vercel privacy policy.
  • Vercel Web Analytics counts page views. It doesn't use cookies and gives us totals, not a record of individual people.
  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces, so your browser requests them from Google, which receives your IP address.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent data, and we don't use advertising or tracking cookies.
  • We don't ask for your LinkedIn login and never post on your behalf.
  • You don't need to give your name or email to use the free plan or an invite code. Signing in is optional.

Why we're allowed to process it

Rewriting your draft is the service you ask for when you press Rewrite, and keeping your account is the service you ask for when you sign in. Counting usage, limiting invite attempts and keeping basic hosting logs are needed to run the free plan fairly and keep the site secure, which is our legitimate interest.

Your choices and rights

  • You can clear this site's cookies and session storage in your browser settings at any time.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to processing of your personal data, and to complain to your data protection authority. Email us and we'll help. Apart from the email address of signed-in users, we keep only a random ID, an IP address and short-lived counts, so there is usually very little to find.

Children

This site isn't meant for anyone under 16.

Changes to this policy

We'll update this page when we change what we collect, for example when Pro accounts and payments launch, and change the date at the top.
